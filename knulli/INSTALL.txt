Snap FE - Alpha Build 1.2.7
for Allwinner H700 Anbernic handhelds running Knulli.
Built and tested on the RG SP and RG35XX-SP; other H700 RG XX devices
(RG35XX/H/Plus/Pro, RG40XX, RG28XX, CubeXX) should work but are untested.


Install or update
-----------------
1. Download the RELEASE ASSET named SnapFE-Alpha-1.2.7.zip. Do not download
   GitHub's automatically generated "Source code" ZIP.

2. BOOT KNULLI ON THE HANDHELD ONCE FIRST, if you have not already.

   There is no SHARE drive on a freshly flashed card. Knulli creates it on
   its first boot: it grows the partition to fill the card and formats it
   exFAT so your computer can read it. Skip this and your PC will show only
   unreadable Linux partitions and no SHARE.

   Let it reach the menu, then power the handheld off.

3. Turn the handheld off and put the Knulli SD card in your computer.

4. Open the drive named SHARE and extract the contents of this ZIP DIRECTLY
   onto SHARE. After extraction, these paths must exist:

      SHARE/system/snapos/snapos_ui
      SHARE/roms/ports/Snap FE (Set As Default).sh

   Choose Merge/Replace if asked. Your settings, favorites, saves, ROMs,
   scraped art, BIOS, and account files are not included in the package and
   are not removed.

5. Safely eject the card, put it back in the handheld, and boot Knulli.

6. In EmulationStation, open PORTS and run:

      Snap FE (Set As Default)

   The installer checks the CPU, loader, glibc, SDL libraries, executable, and
   boot hook. On success it reboots automatically into Snap FE.


Try without installing
----------------------
Run "Snap FE" in PORTS. Quitting Snap FE returns to EmulationStation and does
not change the boot frontend.


If a Port flashes black and returns
-----------------------------------
The repaired launcher records the actual failure instead of silently exiting.
On the SHARE drive, read or send these files:

   system/snapos/LAST-PORT-STATUS.txt
   system/snapos/loader-check.log
   system/snapos/port-launch.log
   system/snapos/install.log

The most common cause with 1.1.5 was extracting the version wrapper folder
instead of putting system/ and roms/ at the root of SHARE. The 1.2.7 ZIP is
flat so "Extract to SHARE" now produces the correct layout automatically.


No SHARE drive on your computer?
-------------------------------
- Most often the card has never been booted in the handheld. See step 2.
- Windows cannot read Linux filesystems, so the other partitions on the card
  will look unreadable or prompt you to format them. That is normal. Ignore
  them and never let Windows format anything.
- Rarely, Knulli's on-device exFAT formatting produces a partition Windows
  sees in Disk Management but will not assign a drive letter to. Repartition
  and format the card with a tool such as MiniTool Partition Wizard, then set
  it as the data source in Knulli again.
- You can skip the card entirely: with SSH or the network share enabled in
  Knulli, copy system/ and roms/ to /userdata over the network instead.


Using a second SD card for games
-------------------------------
Knulli mounts only ONE card as its data drive and leaves the other one
unmounted, so a second card full of games is invisible to any frontend until
something mounts it. Snap FE now does that for you at startup and adds the
card's roms folder to your library automatically.

If the second card is blank, open

   Settings > Games > Storage & Games Folders

pick the card, and choose "Use this card for games". Snap FE creates the
per-system folders on it, copying the names your main card already uses.
The same screen can check for missing folders, mount or eject a card, and
shows how many games are on each.


Restore EmulationStation
------------------------
Run "Snap FE (Restore EmulationStation)" in PORTS and reboot, or delete
SHARE/system/custom.sh. A pre-existing custom.sh is saved as
custom.sh.pre-snapos and restored when available.
