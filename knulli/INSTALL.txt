Snap FE - Alpha Build 1.1.0
for the Anbernic RG34XX-SP / RG34XX running Knulli


What this is
------------
A different menu for your handheld. Knulli still runs everything under the
hood - emulators, controllers, sound, all of it. Snap FE just replaces the
EmulationStation menu with a nicer one.

It's a separate program that runs instead of the ES menu - not a fork of
EmulationStation or Knulli, and it doesn't modify either. It only adds two
things: a folder (system/snapos) and a boot file (system/custom.sh). It never
formats or repartitions the card and never touches your ROMs, saves or BIOS.

Nothing gets overwritten. Your games, saves and settings are left alone, and
you can go back to EmulationStation any time by deleting system/custom.sh.

Still an alpha: it launches RetroArch / libretro games only for now, not every
system Knulli supports, and the artwork is still being filled in.


Installing
----------
1. Turn the handheld off and put the Knulli SD card in your computer.

2. Open the drive called SHARE.

3. Copy the "system" and "roms" folders from this zip onto SHARE. If it asks,
   choose Merge - it only adds files.

4. Eject, put the card back, turn the handheld on.

5. It boots into EmulationStation this one time. Go to PORTS and you'll see:

      Snap FE                        - try it. Quit to come back to ES.
      Snap FE (Set As Default)       - boot straight into Snap FE from now on
      Snap FE (Restore EmulationStation) - undo that

6. Run "Snap FE (Set As Default)" and reboot. Done.


First run
---------
It asks you a few things - which handheld you have, time zone, your city (for
weather and radio), and where your games are. All of it can be changed later
in Settings.

First boot can take a minute. That's normal for this chip. It's quick after.


Going back to EmulationStation
------------------------------
Any one of these:
  - In Snap FE: Settings > Device > "Switch Back to EmulationStation"
  - In Knulli's PORTS menu: "Snap FE (Restore EmulationStation)"
  - On a computer: delete system/custom.sh from SHARE

To remove Snap FE completely, also delete the system/snapos folder and the
three "Snap FE" files in roms/ports.


A few notes
-----------
  - No sound? Reboot once more.
  - Box art: Settings > Account. A free screenscraper.fr login works well.
    Or drop your own art in system/snapos/boxart.
  - Music: put mp3/flac/wav/ogg files in a "music" folder on SHARE.
  - Low battery: Settings > Device has Auto Power Save and Night Mode.

It's an alpha, so expect rough edges. If something breaks, note your Knulli
version, which handheld, and what you were doing.
